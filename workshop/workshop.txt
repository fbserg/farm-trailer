title=Farm Trailer [B42] - RV Interior Add-on
description=[h1]The cows wanted a garden.[/h1]
description=Turn KI5's livestock trailer into a [b]12 × 40 outdoor farm you can tow[/b]. Grow crops, keep animals and build your own little homestead inside. The apocalypse has done very little for property prices.
description=
description=Each trailer gets its own persistent plot with soil, permanent wooden walls and [b]two empty water collectors, 800 L total[/b]. No roof. Normal weather, seasons, animal care and decay apply. Your crops, livestock and belongings stay when you leave.
description=
description=[b]You start with an empty plot.[/b] Bring your own crops, pens and cows. The gameplay photos show a furnished demo.
description=
description=[h2]Set up & get in[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Subscribe to and enable [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3543229299]Project RV Interior[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3330403100]Trailers![/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3171167894]that DAMN Library[/url] and Farm Trailer. Use the current Project RV Interior release (October 26, 2026 or later).
description=[*]Load [b]map_distanciado[/b] before [b]Muldraugh, KY[/b] in the world's map list. No extra Farm Trailer map is needed.
description=[*]Stand beside an [b]unlocked KI5 livestock trailer[/b] and choose the RV Interior entry option in the vehicle radial menu.
description=[*]Inside, right-click the ground to leave.
description=[/olist]
description=This works with KI5's livestock trailer only (Base.TrailerKI5livestock). A trailer that already has an assigned RV interior keeps it. Use one without an assigned interior for a farm.
description=
description=[h2]Water[/h2]
description=Enter once to create the farm. Fill the collectors with rain or normal water containers.
description=
description=[b]To pump from a river or lake:[/b]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Carry a [b]Rubber Hose[/b] and hitch the trailer to a tow vehicle with a charged battery.
description=[*]Park within [b]six tiles[/b] of the water.
description=[*]Choose [b]Fill farm tanks[/b] in the vehicle radial. Walk away to stop.
description=[/olist]
description=The pump adds 20 L at a time. A full 800 L fill uses 20% battery charge. [b]River water stays tainted, and stored water adds towing weight.[/b] The truck has opinions about your new pond.
description=
description=The radial shows the last measured water level. Enter the farm to refresh it. [b]LOW[/b] means less than 160 L.
description=
description=[h2]Animals in, animals out[/h2]
description=[b]The trailer's livestock compartment and the farm are separate.[/b] An animal loaded into the trailer still needs a transfer into the farm.
description=
description=[b]Into the farm[/b]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Load an animal into the trailer with the normal livestock controls.
description=[*]Choose [b]Bring livestock into farm[/b] in the vehicle radial, then select the animal.
description=[/olist]
description=You and that animal enter together. Repeat for each animal. Set up livestock zones, pens, feed and water inside. The cows have declined to help.
description=
description=[b]Out of the farm:[/b] stand near the animal inside, right-click nearby and choose [b]Bring [animal] out of farm[/b]. You both return outside. The animal appears on the ground beside the trailer.
description=
description=The normal livestock panel's [b]Remove Animal[/b] option also unloads onto the ground outside. If a transfer stops with an animal in your hands, place it with the normal animal controls or enter its intended farm to finish. Put it down before you transfer another.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer & compatibility[/h2]
description=For Project Zomboid [b]Build 42.21[/b]. [b]Works in live multiplayer:[/b] tested on the author's own server with real players.The server and every player need Farm Trailer and all three dependencies.

description=[*]Restart the server and clients after updates.
description=[/list]
description=[b]No entry option?[/b] Check enabled mods on both server and clients, the map order and whether the trailer already has an interior.

description=There are [b]38 farm plots per world[/b]. The perimeter blocks climbing, attacks, fire and sledgehammers. Avoid other add-ons that replace this trailer's interior or use the same RV Interior map space. Back up your save before a mod change.
